some may historically correspond to kosar, noor, or caspian, whose codes were not recovered.Known ambiguous casesShared BINs and sub-BIN splits for digital brands represent the highest-risk routing ambiguity for card validators. Under Saman Bank (saman, Sheba 056), official Blu Bank documentation (blubank.com) states that Blu cards are issued under Saman Bank's card prefix, while Tier 3 lists (banky.blogsky.com and gist.github.com/hasanparasteh) isolate Blu Bank (blu) via digits 7–8 equal to 19 (62198619). Validators should therefore match blu on 62198619 and fall back to saman for all other 621986 cards, keeping in mind that Saman could allocate additional sub-BINs in the future. Similarly, under Bank Pasargad (pasargad, Sheba 057), a single Tier 3 blog (banky.blogsky.com) splits BIN 502229 using digits 7–8 equal to 15 (50222915) for WePod (wepod), whereas Pasargad's youth digital brand ZPod (زیپاد) also uses Sheba 057 with no documented sub-BIN. For Middle East Bank (middle-east, Sheba 078), user reports on way2pay.ir indicate that its digital brand Bankino (بانکینو) shares the 5859 (585947) prefix without a verified sub-BIN split, so Bankino is not split into a duplicate row.A second recurring source of misidentification is truncating card prefixes to 4 digits instead of matching the full 6-digit BIN. Multiple unrelated banks share identical 4-digit openings: 6037 spans Bank Melli (603799), Bank Saderat (603769), and Bank Keshavarzi (603770); 5859 spans Bank Tejarat (585983) and Middle East Bank (585947); 5029 spans Tose'e Ta'avon (502908), Karafarin (502910), and Bank Day (502938); 6393 spans Bank Sina (639346), Bank Pasargad (639347), and legacy Mehr Eghtesad (639370); 6273 spans Bank Tejarat (627353) and legacy Ansar Bank (627381); and 6274 spans Eghtesad Novin (627412) and Karafarin (627488). Production validators must never infer an Iranian bank from fewer than 6 digits.Post-merger Sheba migration and legacy card circulation create overlapping historical and active identifiers across three major transitions. First, following the 2019–2020 consolidation of Ansar (063, 627381), Ghavamin (052, 639599), Hekmat Iranian (065, 636949), Mehr Eghtesad (079, 639370), and Kosar (505801) into Bank Sepah, migrated accounts received Bank Sepah Sheba numbers under code 015 (with pishkhanak.com explicitly noting that IRxx079 is deactivated), while legacy cards and historical IBAN records remain in circulation and should resolve as merged into sepah. Second, following Ayandeh Bank's CBI dissolution decision effective 1 Aban 1404 (2025-10-23) and the transfer of depositors and branches to Bank Melli Iran starting Saturday 3 Aban 1404 (2025-10-25), snn.ir reported that existing Ayandeh cards, account numbers, and legacy systems remain operational alongside Bank Melli's systems. Validators must therefore retain 636214 and 062 as detectable legacy identifiers mapped to mergedInto: "melli", alongside 507677 for Noor Credit Institution (whose deposits transferred to Bank Melli in Bahman 1402 / February 2024). Third, Askariyeh Credit Institution rebranded to Melal Credit Institution while retaining Sheba code 075 and card BIN 606256; both codes are assigned solely to the active melal row while askariyeh is preserved as a rebranded alias record pointing to melal to avoid duplicate primary keys.Two final naming and attribution collisions require defensive handling. In Sheba lookup tables, rade.ir/sheba/ misassigns code 078 to Iran-Venezuela Bi-National Bank, contradicting zarinpal.com, vandarpay.github.io, tgju.org, and banky.blogsky.com, all of which assign 078 to Middle East Bank and 095 to Iran-Venezuela Bi-National Bank.
