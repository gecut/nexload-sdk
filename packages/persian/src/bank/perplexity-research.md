**Release decision: do not hard-code this as a production allowlist.** The JSON includes all 30 entries observed across the three pages of the [CBI licensing roster](https://cbi.ir/BanksInstitutions/BankInstitute.aspx), plus 17 historical or digital-brand entries. The current roster was enumerated, but historical completeness and production-grade identifier coverage were not established. Each populated identifier is a research candidate unless independently cleared for release. `null` or `[]` means evidence was insufficient, not that the institution never had that identifier. [cbi](https://cbi.ir/BanksInstitutions/BankInstitute.aspx)

`mergedInto` means the recorded successor or claims administrator; it does not assert a corporate merger when `status` is `dissolved` or `rebranded`. For Ayandeh, the schema’s `dissolved` value represents a non-operating former bank in resolution, and `statusDate` is the license-revocation date, not a finding that corporate liquidation had finished. For legacy records, `legacy` is carried in `cardBins[].note` rather than added as an undeclared field. [cbi](https://cbi.ir/showitem/33658.aspx)
