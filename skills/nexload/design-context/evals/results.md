## Final adversarial review

A separate reviewer reproduced three additional defects using temporary synthetic bundles:
legacy superseded requirements were being rejected instead of retained; previous derived context
could falsely resolve an unknown; a removed page could still remain active. Together with the
cross-view drift limitation, these were corrected and covered by regression tests. Regenerated
claims may keep stable IDs after invalidating prior versions, with change notes; removed pages may
not remain active.

Semantic entailment, truthful source ownership, complete route/state discovery, remote visual/runtime
inspection and generalization across real large repositories remain judgment-dependent. JSON/schema
success is not proof that those claims are true. The mandatory evidence audit, explicit coverage,
source hashes, unknowns and computed readiness reduce these risks without claiming to eliminate them.
